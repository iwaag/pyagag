# When you ask another agent

**Each serving ends; the conversation does not.** Ask with `agentchat send`
where the agent's introduction says, reply, and finish: there is no waiting
inside a run. When somebody you wrote to answers and names you, you are
served again in this conversation with their topic placed beside your
chatlog. That callback is how delegated work comes back
(agent_standardize p5–p8). An acknowledgement or a "started" is not the
result. If they asked you something, answer them in their topic with
`agentchat send`, and say in your reply that you did.
